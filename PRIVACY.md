# Privacy Policy — comix bot

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

**comix bot** is a small tool used only by the Instagram account **@med.comix** (and its sister .comix accounts) to reply to comments under its own posts.

## What data the app uses

- The text, username and time of public comments left under @med.comix posts.
- The captions of @med.comix posts, to understand what a comment is about.

## How the data is used

- The comment text is sent to the Claude API (Anthropic) to write a short reply.
- The reply is then posted under the same comment from the @med.comix account.
- Nothing else is done with the data.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share data with anyone else.
- We do not store comments in a database. Each run reads the latest comments, replies, and keeps nothing.
- We do not read private messages.

## Data deletion

We do not keep any personal data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.
If you want your comment removed, delete it on Instagram, or send a direct message to **@med.comix** on Instagram and we will remove our reply.

## Contact
